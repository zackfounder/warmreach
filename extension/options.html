Autonomous LinkedIn Outreach

Free and open source. Everything stays on this computer: no account, no server, no AI, no API keys. You must be logged in to LinkedIn in this browser.

2. Who to find

It only looks at people who are not connected to you yet (2nd and 3rd degree), and saves everyone it finds so you can export them.

3. What it says

Connection requests go out with no note. When someone accepts, they get one of your first messages. Versions are used in turn, so Results shows which one gets answered. Use {first_name}, {company}, {my_first_name} and {calendar_link}. A version that needs something the person does not have (like a company) is skipped for them.

4. How fast

Limits start at 25% on day one and rise to the full number over 14 days. It waits 3 to 9 minutes between invitations and messages. Higher numbers raise the risk of LinkedIn restricting your account.
